Chia Energy Balls

EnergyHealthy snackOmega-3sFiber

These no-bake energy balls are perfect for a quick, healthy snack. They're naturally sweetened with dates and packed with nutrients from chia seeds.

Perfect On-the-Go Snack

These energy balls are:

  • No baking required
  • Easy to make in large batches
  • Perfect for meal prep
  • Great for kids and adults
  • Naturally sweet without refined sugar

Storage

Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator. They'll keep for up to 2 weeks, making them perfect for meal prep.

Ingredients

  • •2 tablespoons Chia seeds(Shop)
  • •1 cup, pitted Dates
  • •1/2 cup Almonds
  • •1/4 cup Coconut flakes
  • •2 tablespoons Cocoa powder
  • •1/2 teaspoon Vanilla extract
  • •1 pinch Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. 1Soak dates in warm water for 15 minutes if they're hard
  2. 2In a food processor, pulse almonds until coarsely ground
  3. 3Add soaked dates, chia seeds, coconut flakes, cocoa powder, vanilla, and salt
  4. 4Process until mixture comes together and forms a ball
  5. 5If too dry, add a teaspoon of water. If too wet, add more coconut flakes
  6. 6Roll mixture into 1-inch balls
  7. 7Store in refrigerator for up to 2 weeks
