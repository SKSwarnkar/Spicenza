Golden Turmeric Latte

Anti-inflammatoryDigestive supportImmune supportWarming and comforting

This warming golden latte is a delicious way to enjoy the benefits of turmeric. The combination of turmeric, black pepper, and healthy fats from coconut milk helps maximize the absorption of curcumin, turmeric's active compound.

Why This Recipe Works

The key to getting the most from turmeric is combining it with black pepper and healthy fats. Black pepper contains piperine, which enhances curcumin absorption by up to 2000%. The fats in coconut milk also help your body absorb the fat-soluble curcumin.

Customization Tips

  • Use almond milk or oat milk if you prefer
  • Add a dash of vanilla extract for extra flavor
  • For a caffeine-free option, this is perfect any time of day
  • Adjust sweetness to your preference
  • Add a pinch of cardamom for an extra Ayurvedic touch

When to Enjoy

This latte is perfect for:

  • Morning routine
  • Afternoon pick-me-up
  • Evening wind-down (caffeine-free)
  • Cold weather comfort
  • When you need an anti-inflammatory boost

Ingredients

  • •1 teaspoon Turmeric powder(Shop)
  • •1 cup Coconut milk
  • •1-2 teaspoons Honey or maple syrup
  • •Pinch Black pepper
  • •1/4 teaspoon Cinnamon
  • •1/4 teaspoon Ginger powder (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Heat coconut milk in a small saucepan over medium heat until warm (do not boil)
  2. 2Whisk in turmeric powder, cinnamon, and ginger (if using) until well combined
  3. 3Add honey or maple syrup to taste
  4. 4Add a pinch of black pepper (this helps with curcumin absorption)
  5. 5Continue whisking until frothy and well mixed
  6. 6Pour into your favorite mug and enjoy warm
