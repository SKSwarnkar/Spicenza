Amla Immunity Juice

Immune supportVitamin C boostDigestive healthEnergy

This traditional Ayurvedic drink is a powerful way to start your day. Amla is one of the richest natural sources of Vitamin C, making this juice an excellent immune support drink.

Why This Works

Amla contains 20 times more Vitamin C than oranges, making it one of the most potent natural sources of this essential vitamin. Combined with lemon and honey, this juice provides a natural energy boost and immune support.

Best Time to Drink

Traditionally consumed on an empty stomach in the morning, this juice helps kickstart your metabolism and provides a natural energy boost without caffeine.

Ingredients

  • •1 teaspoon Amla powder(Shop)
  • •1 cup Water
  • •1-2 teaspoons Honey
  • •1/2 lemon Lemon juice
  • •Pinch Ginger powder (optional)(Shop)

Instructions

  1. 1Warm 1 cup of water (not boiling)
  2. 2Add amla powder and stir until dissolved
  3. 3Add honey and lemon juice
  4. 4Optionally add a pinch of ginger powder for extra warmth
  5. 5Stir well and drink on an empty stomach in the morning
