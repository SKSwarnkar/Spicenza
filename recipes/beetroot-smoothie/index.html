Beetroot Energy Smoothie

Energy boostCardiovascular healthIron supportPre-workout

This vibrant smoothie is perfect for a morning energy boost or pre-workout fuel. Beetroot powder provides natural nitrates that support healthy blood flow and energy.

Natural Energy

The nitrates in beetroot are converted to nitric oxide in your body, which supports healthy circulation and can enhance athletic performance. This makes it an excellent pre-workout smoothie.

Beautiful Color

Beetroot powder gives this smoothie a naturally vibrant pink color that's both beautiful and nutritious. The berries help mask any earthy flavor while adding their own antioxidants.

Ingredients

  • •1 tablespoon Beetroot powder(Shop)
  • •1 medium, frozen Banana
  • •1/2 cup, frozen Berries
  • •1/2 cup Greek yogurt or plant-based yogurt
  • •1 cup Almond milk
  • •1 teaspoon Honey or maple syrup

Instructions

  1. 1Add almond milk to blender
  2. 2Add frozen banana and berries
  3. 3Add yogurt and beetroot powder
  4. 4Add sweetener if desired
  5. 5Blend until smooth and creamy
  6. 6The beetroot powder will give it a beautiful pink color
