Spiced Superfood Oatmeal

FiberAnti-inflammatoryBlood sugar supportWarming breakfast

This warming oatmeal is supercharged with anti-inflammatory spices. Perfect for a healthy, satisfying breakfast that supports your wellness goals.

Spice Benefits

This oatmeal combines three powerful spices:

  • Cinnamon: Blood sugar support and warming
  • Turmeric: Anti-inflammatory support
  • Ginger: Digestive support and warmth

Customization

Feel free to add:

  • Different fruits (berries, apples, etc.)
  • Various nuts and seeds
  • Superfood powders (moringa, matcha, etc.)
  • Different plant milks
  • Nut butters for extra protein

Ingredients

  • •1/2 cup Rolled oats
  • •1/2 teaspoon Cinnamon powder(Shop)
  • •1/4 teaspoon Turmeric powder(Shop)
  • •1/4 teaspoon Ginger powder(Shop)
  • •1 cup Water or plant milk
  • •1-2 tablespoons Honey or maple syrup
  • •1/2, sliced Banana
  • •2 tablespoons Nuts or seeds

Instructions

  1. 1In a small saucepan, combine oats and liquid
  2. 2Add cinnamon, turmeric, and ginger powders
  3. 3Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 5-7 minutes
  4. 4Stir occasionally until oats are creamy
  5. 5Remove from heat and stir in sweetener
  6. 6Top with sliced banana and nuts or seeds
  7. 7Enjoy warm
