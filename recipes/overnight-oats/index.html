Superfood Overnight Oats

FiberProteinHealthy breakfastMake-ahead meal

Overnight oats are the perfect make-ahead breakfast. This version is supercharged with chia seeds and moringa for extra nutrition.

Why Overnight Oats?

Overnight oats are:

  • Ready when you wake up
  • Creamy and delicious
  • Highly customizable
  • Packed with fiber and protein
  • Perfect for busy mornings

Customization Ideas

  • Add different superfood powders (matcha, spirulina, etc.)
  • Use different plant milks
  • Add various fruits and toppings
  • Mix in nut butters for extra protein
  • Add spices like cinnamon or cardamom

Ingredients

  • •1/2 cup Rolled oats
  • •1 tablespoon Chia seeds(Shop)
  • •3/4 cup Almond milk
  • •1 teaspoon Moringa powder(Shop)
  • •1-2 tablespoons Honey or maple syrup
  • •1/2, sliced Banana
  • •1/4 cup Berries
  • •2 tablespoons Nuts (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1In a jar or bowl, combine oats and chia seeds
  2. 2Add moringa powder and mix well
  3. 3Add almond milk and sweetener, stir to combine
  4. 4Cover and refrigerate overnight (at least 6 hours)
  5. 5In the morning, stir well and add sliced banana and berries
  6. 6Top with nuts if desired
  7. 7Enjoy cold or let sit at room temperature for 10 minutes
