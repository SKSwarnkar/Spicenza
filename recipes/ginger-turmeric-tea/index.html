Ginger Turmeric Tea

Anti-inflammatoryDigestive supportImmune supportWarming

This warming tea combines two powerful anti-inflammatory spices. Perfect for cold weather, digestive support, or when you need an immune boost.

Powerful Combination

Turmeric: Contains curcumin, a powerful anti-inflammatory compound Ginger: Contains gingerol, also anti-inflammatory and great for digestion

Together, these spices create a potent wellness drink that's been used in traditional medicine for thousands of years.

Best Times to Enjoy

  • Morning routine
  • After meals for digestive support
  • When feeling under the weather
  • Cold weather comfort
  • Evening wind-down (caffeine-free)

Ingredients

  • •1 teaspoon Turmeric powder(Shop)
  • •1/2 teaspoon Ginger powder(Shop)
  • •2 cups Water
  • •Pinch Black pepper
  • •To taste Honey or lemon (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Bring water to a boil in a small saucepan
  2. 2Reduce heat and add turmeric and ginger powders
  3. 3Add a pinch of black pepper (enhances curcumin absorption)
  4. 4Simmer for 5-7 minutes
  5. 5Strain into a mug
  6. 6Add honey and/or lemon juice if desired
  7. 7Enjoy warm
