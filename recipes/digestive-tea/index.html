Digestive Support Tea

Digestive supportWarmingBlood sugar supportComfort

This soothing tea combines warming spices that support healthy digestion. Perfect after meals or when you need digestive comfort.

Digestive Support

Both ginger and cinnamon have been traditionally used to support healthy digestion. Ginger helps reduce nausea and supports digestive function, while cinnamon may help maintain healthy blood sugar levels.

Warming Properties

This tea is perfect for:

  • After heavy meals
  • Cold weather
  • Digestive discomfort
  • General wellness support

Ingredients

  • •1/2 teaspoon Ginger powder(Shop)
  • •1/2 teaspoon Cinnamon powder(Shop)
  • •2 cups Water
  • •1-2 teaspoons Honey
  • •1/2 lemon Lemon juice

Instructions

  1. 1Bring water to a boil
  2. 2Add ginger and cinnamon powders
  3. 3Reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes
  4. 4Remove from heat and let steep for 2-3 minutes
  5. 5Strain into a mug
  6. 6Add honey and lemon juice
  7. 7Stir and enjoy warm
