b:T798,<h2>What it is</h2>
<p>Jowar, also known as sorghum, is a traditional Indian grain that has been cultivated for thousands of years. It's a staple in many parts of India and is known for its versatility and nutritional benefits. Jowar is naturally gluten-free and is an excellent source of fiber, protein, and essential minerals.</p>
<h2>Taste &#x26; aroma notes</h2>
<p>Jowar has a mild, slightly nutty flavor with a subtle sweetness. When processed into snacks like chivda or crisps, it develops a light, airy texture that's both satisfying and easy to digest. The grain itself has a neutral flavor profile that pairs well with a variety of spices and seasonings.</p>
<h2>How to use</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>In snacks</strong>: Jowar is commonly used in traditional Indian snacks like chivda, poha, and various crispy treats</li>
<li><strong>As flour</strong>: Ground jowar flour can be used in rotis, breads, and baked goods</li>
<li><strong>As whole grain</strong>: Cooked jowar can be used as a rice alternative or added to soups and stews</li>
<li><strong>In breakfast</strong>: Jowar flakes can be used in porridge or as a cereal base</li>
</ul>
<h2>Quality notes</h2>
<p>When selecting jowar-based products, look for:</p>
<ul>
<li>Whole grain varieties that retain the bran and germ</li>
<li>Products that are minimally processed to preserve nutritional value</li>
<li>Fresh, clean aroma without any musty or off odors</li>
<li>Proper storage in cool, dry conditions to maintain freshness</li>
</ul>
<h2>What we do not claim</h2>
<p>This information is for educational purposes only. Jowar is a traditional food grain and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Individual nutritional needs may vary, and we recommend consulting with a healthcare provider for personalized dietary advice.</p>
