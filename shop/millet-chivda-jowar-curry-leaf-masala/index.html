Millet Chivda (Jowar) | Curry Leaf Masala

Traditional chivda made from jowar millet, seasoned with aromatic curry leaves and masala spices. A gluten-free, whole grain snack with a unique South Indian flavor profile.

Millet Chivda is a traditional Indian snack mix that combines the wholesome goodness of jowar (sorghum) with aromatic spices and curry leaves. Our Curry Leaf Masala variety brings together the distinctive flavor of curry leaves with a balanced masala blend, creating a snack that's both familiar and unique.

Traditional Chivda with a Twist

Chivda is a beloved Indian snack mix that's been enjoyed for generations. Our version uses jowar millet as the base, providing a gluten-free, whole grain alternative to traditional chivda. The millet is carefully processed to create a light, crispy texture that's perfect for snacking.

The Aroma of Curry Leaves

Curry leaves are a staple in South Indian cuisine, known for their distinctive, aromatic flavor. When combined with our masala blend, they create a unique taste profile that's both earthy and refreshing. The curry leaf aroma is subtle but present, adding depth to every bite.

Whole Grain Goodness

Jowar (sorghum) is a traditional Indian grain that's naturally gluten-free and rich in fiber. When made into chivda, it creates a snack that's both satisfying and nutritious. The whole grain base provides sustained energy while the spices add flavor without excess calories.

Perfect for Any Time

Whether you're looking for a tea-time snack, an evening treat, or a healthy alternative to processed snacks, Millet Chivda with Curry Leaf Masala delivers. The convenient packaging makes it easy to enjoy on the go or share with family and friends.
