<h2>Traditional Chivda with a Twist</h2>

<h2>The Aroma of Curry Leaves</h2>
<p>Curry leaves are a staple in South Indian cuisine, known for their distinctive, aromatic flavor. When combined with our masala blend, they create a unique taste profile that's both earthy and refreshing. The curry leaf aroma is subtle but present, adding depth to every bite.</p>
<h2>Whole Grain Goodness</h2>
<p>Jowar (sorghum) is a traditional Indian grain that's naturally gluten-free and rich in fiber. When made into chivda, it creates a snack that's both satisfying and nutritious. The whole grain base provides sustained energy while the spices add flavor without excess calories.</p>
