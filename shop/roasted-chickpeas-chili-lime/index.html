Roasted Chickpeas | Chili Lime

Crispy roasted chickpeas with zesty chili lime seasoning. A protein-packed snack with a bold, tangy kick.

Roasted chickpeas are a protein powerhouse, and when seasoned with our zesty chili lime blend, they become an irresistible snack. The combination of protein, fiber, and bold flavor makes this a perfect choice for those who want nutrition without compromising on taste.

Protein Power

Chickpeas are naturally rich in protein and fiber, making them an excellent choice for sustained energy and satiety. When roasted to perfection, they develop a satisfying crunch that's hard to resist.

Zesty & Bold

The chili lime seasoning brings together the bright, tangy flavor of lime with the gentle warmth of chili. This creates a flavor profile that's both refreshing and satisfying, perfect for those who enjoy snacks with personality.

Versatile & Delicious

These roasted chickpeas are perfect on their own, but they also make a great addition to salads, soups, or any dish that could use a protein boost and some crunch.
