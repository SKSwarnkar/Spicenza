Roasted Makhana | Turmeric Pepper

Golden roasted makhana seasoned with turmeric and black pepper. A wellness-focused snack that combines great taste with traditional Ayurvedic benefits.

2 oz5 oz

Turmeric and black pepper are a classic Ayurvedic combination, and when paired with roasted makhana, they create a snack that's both delicious and wellness-focused. The black pepper enhances the absorption of curcumin from turmeric, making this a smart choice for those seeking the benefits of this golden spice.

The Turmeric & Pepper Advantage

Turmeric is known for its warming properties and active compound curcumin, while black pepper contains piperine, which enhances curcumin absorption. Together, they create a flavor profile that's both satisfying and beneficial.

Golden Goodness

The turmeric gives our makhana a beautiful golden hue and a distinctive earthy flavor, while the black pepper adds a gentle warmth that complements the natural nuttiness of the lotus seeds. This combination creates a snack that's as visually appealing as it is delicious.

Wellness in Every Bite

Perfect for those who want to incorporate more turmeric into their diet in a convenient, enjoyable way. Each serving provides the benefits of turmeric and black pepper in a snack format that's easy to enjoy throughout the day.
