Organic Spirulina Powder

Blue-green algae superfood rich in protein, B vitamins, and iron. One of the most nutrient-dense foods on Earth.

Spirulina is often called a "superfood" and for good reason. This blue-green algae is one of the most nutrient-dense foods on the planet, containing an impressive array of vitamins, minerals, and protein.

Ancient Superfood

Spirulina has been consumed for thousands of years. The Aztecs harvested it from Lake Texcoco, and it's been a traditional food source in Chad for centuries. Today, we understand why this ancient food has stood the test of time.

Protein Powerhouse

What makes spirulina unique is its protein content. By weight, spirulina is 60-70% protein, making it one of the best plant-based protein sources available. It contains all essential amino acids, making it a complete protein.

Rich in B Vitamins

Spirulina is particularly rich in B vitamins, including B12, which is often lacking in plant-based diets. This makes it an excellent supplement for vegetarians and vegans.

Quality and Purity

Our organic spirulina is carefully cultivated in controlled environments to ensure purity and quality. We test for contaminants and ensure our product meets the highest standards for safety and potency.
