<h2>Ancient Superfood</h2>
<p>Spirulina has been consumed for thousands of years. The Aztecs harvested it from Lake Texcoco, and it's been a traditional food source in Chad for centuries. Today, we understand why this ancient food has stood the test of time.</p>
<h2>Protein Powerhouse</h2>
<p>What makes spirulina unique is its protein content. By weight, spirulina is 60-70% protein, making it one of the best plant-based protein sources available. It contains all essential amino acids, making it a complete protein.</p>
<h2>Rich in B Vitamins</h2>
<p>Spirulina is particularly rich in B vitamins, including B12, which is often lacking in plant-based diets. This makes it an excellent supplement for vegetarians and vegans.</p>
