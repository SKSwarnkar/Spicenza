<h2>Why Makhana?</h2>
<p>Makhana seeds are naturally gluten-free, low in calories, and rich in protein and fiber. They're a perfect alternative to processed snacks, offering a satisfying crunch without the guilt. <p>Our Classic Masala seasoning is a carefully balanced blend of traditional Indian spices including cumin, coriander, turmeric, and warming spices.
